        S1(n;x)=∑i=1nxi=x−xn+11−x S_1(n; x) = \sum_{i=1}^n x^i = \frac{x - x^{n + 1}}{1 - x} S1​(n;x)=i=1∑n​xi=1−xx−xn+1​

        则记

        S2(n;x)=S1′(n;x)=∑i=1nixi−1=(1−(n+1)xn)(1−x)−(x−xn+1)(−1)(1−x)2=1−(n+1)xn+nxn+1(1−x)2 \begin{aligned} S_2(n; x) &= S_1'(n; x) \\ &= \sum_{i=1}^n i x^{i - 1} \\ &= \frac{(1 - (n + 1)x^n)(1 - x) - (x - x^{n + 1})(-1)}{(1 - x)^2} \\ &= \frac{1 - (n + 1)x^n + nx^{n + 1}}{(1 - x)^2} \end{aligned} S2​(n;x)​=S1′​(n;x)=i=1∑n​ixi−1=(1−x)2(1−(n+1)xn)(1−x)−(x−xn+1)(−1)​=(1−x)21−(n+1)xn+nxn+1​​

        即得证.

        注意到

        ∑i=1ni2xi−1=∑i=1n(i(i−1)+i)xi−1=x∑i=1ni(i−1)xi−2+∑i=1nixi−1=xS3(n;x)+S2(n;x) \begin{aligned} \sum_{i=1}^n i^2 x^{i-1} &= \sum_{i=1}^n (i(i - 1) + i) x^{i - 1} \\ &= x\sum_{i=1}^n i(i - 1)x^{i - 2} + \sum_{i=1}^n i x^{i - 1} \\ &= x S_3(n; x) + S_2(n; x) \end{aligned} i=1∑n​i2xi−1​=i=1∑n​(i(i−1)+i)xi−1=xi=1∑n​i(i−1)xi−2+i=1∑n​ixi−1=xS3​(n;x)+S2​(n;x)​

      Prop. 1.3 (组合恒等式) 对于 ∀n∈N+\forall n \in \mathbb{N}^+∀n∈N+

      ∑i=1ni(ni)=n2n−1∑i=1ni2(ni)=n(n+1)2n−2 \begin{aligned} \sum_{i=1}^n i \left(\begin{matrix} n \\ i \end{matrix} \right) &= n 2^{n - 1} \\ \sum_{i=1}^n i^2 \left(\begin{matrix} n \\ i \end{matrix} \right) &= n (n + 1) 2^{n - 2} \end{aligned} i=1∑n​i(ni​)i=1∑n​i2(ni​)​=n2n−1=n(n+1)2n−2​
      Proof:

      • (1) 根据组合恒等式，∀n∈N\forall n \in \mathbb{N}∀n∈N

        i⋅(ni)≡n⋅(n−1i−1) i \cdot \left(\begin{matrix} n \\ i \end{matrix} \right) \equiv n \cdot \left(\begin{matrix} n - 1 \\ i - 1 \end{matrix} \right) i⋅(ni​)≡n⋅(n−1i−1​)

        则

        ∑i=1ni(ni)=∑i=1nn(n−1i−1)=n∑i=0n−1(n−1i)=n(x+1)n−1∣x=1=n2n−1 \begin{aligned} \sum_{i=1}^n i \left(\begin{matrix} n \\ i \end{matrix} \right) &= \sum_{i=1}^n n \left(\begin{matrix} n - 1 \\ i - 1 \end{matrix} \right) \\ &= n \sum_{i=0}^{n - 1} \left(\begin{matrix} n - 1 \\ i \end{matrix} \right) \\ &= \left.n(x + 1)^{n - 1}\right|_{x = 1} = n 2^{n - 1} \end{aligned} i=1∑n​i(ni​)​=i=1∑n​n(n−1i−1​)=ni=0∑n−1​(n−1i​)=n(x+1)n−1​x=1​=n2n−1​

      • (2) 根据恒等式和 (1)，有

        ∑i=1ni2(ni)=∑i=1nni(n−1i−1)=n∑i=0n−1(i+1)(n−1i)=n(∑i=0n−1i(n−1i)+∑i=0n−1(n−1i))=n((n−1)2n−2+2n−1)=n(n+1)2n−2 \begin{aligned} \sum_{i=1}^n i^2 \left(\begin{matrix} n \\ i \end{matrix} \right) &= \sum_{i=1}^n ni \left(\begin{matrix} n - 1 \\ i - 1 \end{matrix} \right) \\ &= n \sum_{i=0}^{n - 1} (i + 1) \left(\begin{matrix} n - 1 \\ i \end{matrix} \right) \\ &= n \left(\sum_{i=0}^{n - 1} i \left(\begin{matrix} n - 1 \\ i \end{matrix} \right) + \sum_{i=0}^{n - 1} \left(\begin{matrix} n - 1 \\ i \end{matrix} \right) \right) \\ &= n ( (n - 1)2^{n-2} + 2^{n - 1} ) \\ &= n(n + 1) 2^{n - 2} \end{aligned} i=1∑n​i2(ni​)​=i=1∑n​ni(n−1i−1​)=ni=0∑n−1​(i+1)(n−1i​)=n(i=0∑n−1​i(n−1i​)+i=0∑n−1​(n−1i​))=n((n−1)2n−2+2n−1)=n(n+1)2n−2​

        即原式. □\square□
